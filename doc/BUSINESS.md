## 1. Executive Summary

AetherLive is an AI-native customer support platform that lets any website add a smart support widget with a single `<script>` tag. End customers get instant AI-powered responses, agents manage conversations from a unified dashboard, and businesses retain full control over branding, knowledge base, and integrations.

The global customer support software market is valued at **$10+ billion (₹83,000+ crore)** and growing at ~20% CAGR, driven by rising customer expectations for instant, 24/7 support and the rapid adoption of AI-powered automation. AetherLive targets the underserved mid-market segment where existing solutions are either too expensive (Zendesk, Intercom) or too technically complex to set up.

### Key Market Drivers

- **Remote-first support teams:** Cloud-based, async-first tools are replacing on-premise call center software.

## 5. Value Proposition

### For the Business Owner

- **One script, instant support** — No engineering sprints, no SDK integration guides, no months of setup.
- **Cut support costs by 40–60%** — AI handles 70%+ of routine queries; human agents focus on complex issues.
- **Never miss a customer** — 24/7 AI coverage, even outside business hours.
- **Full context from day one** — Rich metadata (device, location, browser, referral source) captured automatically.

### For the Support Agent

- **Unified inbox** — All conversations (chat, voice, email) in one place.
- **AI-assisted replies** — Suggested responses, knowledge base lookup, auto-tagging.
- **Status management** — Track, filter, and resolve conversations efficiently.
- **Voice calls inside the platform** — No separate phone system needed.

### For the End Customer

- **Instant answers** — AI responds in under 3 seconds.
- **No sign-up required** — Start a conversation anonymously.
- **Multi-channel** — Chat or voice, whichever they prefer.
- **Seamless handoff** — AI to human with full conversation context preserved.

## 8. Key Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| **AI quality insufficient** | High churn | Medium | Fallback to human-only mode; continuous fine-tuning; support GPT-4o, Claude, and local models |
| **Price competition** | Margin compression | Medium | Differentiate on simplicity + developer experience; avoid race-to-bottom |
| **Enterprise trust barrier** | Slow enterprise adoption | High | SOC 2 compliance (plan for Year 2); data residency options; SSO/SAML |
| **Customer churn (SMB)** | Low LTV | High | Invest in onboarding; proactive CS outreach at 30/60/90 days |
| **Vapi dependency (voice)** | Single point of failure | Medium | Abstract voice layer; support Twilio, Vonage as alternatives |
| **LLM API cost overruns** | Negative unit economics | Low | Cache common responses; set per-conversation AI spend limits; monitor token usage |

## 9. Growth Model

### North Star Metric

**Resolved conversations per day** — measures both customer value (issue solved) and platform value (AI + agent productivity).

### Key Growth Levers

1. **Activation** — Time from `<script>` install to first AI response. Target: <10 minutes.
2. **Retention** — % of teams still active after 30/60/90 days. Target: >80% at 90 days.
3. **Referral** — Viral coefficient from agent → other teams. Target: k > 0.3.
4. **Expansion** — Free → Starter → Growth upgrade rate. Target: 15% monthly.
5. **AI performance** — AI resolution rate (%, without human escalation). Target: >70%.

### Expansion Revenue Path

```
Free (single agent, try the product)
  │
  ▼
Starter ($49 / ₹4,067 — small team, basic AI)
  │
  ▼
Growth ($199 / ₹16,517 — growing team, voice, analytics)
  │
  ▼
Business ($799 / ₹66,317 — full team, SLA, priority)
  │
  ▼
Enterprise (custom — dedicated infra, SSO, on-prem)
```
